# Launch Logo Validation

The Launch university landing page used a white-and-gold transparent LevelNext logo directly on a warm ivory navigation background. The white “Level” lettering consequently had insufficient contrast.

The entry-page header now places the existing logo asset in a navy contrast tile with a defined border and responsive dimensions. Desktop and 375-pixel mobile verification at the `/launch` route confirm that the complete LevelNext mark is clear and legible in the top-left corner.

The authenticated Launch platform shell now uses a crisp text lockup with a cropped official emblem instead of scaling down the full raster logo. Desktop and 375-pixel mobile verification at `/launch/home` confirm that the LevelNext mark remains clear in the top-left platform header.

The Launch landing-page footer now uses the same navy contrast tile as the entry-page header. Full-page desktop and 375-pixel mobile verification confirm that the white-and-gold LevelNext asset remains legible against the warm ivory footer background.
